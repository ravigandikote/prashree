# Recording the tones for the website

Monica — this is what the site needs from each recording, so the sound
layer works well on phones and the tones loop without a click. Send the
files to Ravi; the compression and upload happen on our side.

## Tones for individual artworks

- **Length:** 20 to 30 seconds.
- **One bowl, one strike.** Let it ring out and fade naturally. Do not stop
  it, do not layer bowls, no music underneath, no voice.
- **Let it end in silence.** The site loops each tone, so a recording that
  fades fully to quiet before it ends will loop without a seam. A recording
  that is cut off while still ringing will click every time it repeats.
- **Mono is fine.** It halves the file size and nobody will hear the
  difference on a phone.
- **Format:** WAV, or whatever high-quality file your recorder produces. We
  compress it for the web ourselves. Do not send an MP3 you made in another
  app; we get a better result from the original.
- **File name:** the artwork it pairs with, for example `Aditya.wav`.
- **Size after we compress it:** under 300 KB per tone. A 25-second mono
  recording lands comfortably inside that, so you do not need to think about
  file size, only length.

## The gong

- **One strike, 6 to 10 seconds** including the decay.
- Slightly deeper and fuller than the artwork tones. This is a bell that
  opens and closes a practice, not a background sound.
- **Two versions if you can:** an opening strike, and a softer one for the
  close. If you only record one, we use it for both.

## Credits

For every recording, a line or two we can show under the artwork:

- the instrument (for example "Tibetan singing bowl", "crystal bowl")
- the note or key, if you know it
- where and when it was recorded

Example: *Singing bowl in C — Monica Prakash. Recorded at NeeRav Arts
Village, Bengaluru, September 2026.*

## How it will appear on the site

Sound is off until a visitor switches it on in the site header. Once they
do, an artwork with a tone plays it quietly under the page, with the credit
line and a pause control beneath the picture. Artworks without a tone show
nothing extra. The gong plays once at the start and once at the end of
"Two minutes of stillness". Everything works in silence too.
